# Platform checkpoints

Platform pages change frequently. Prefer labels, accessible roles, nearby field text, and post-action readback over fixed screen coordinates.

## Douyin

- Verify the uploaded video remains attached after any recovery.
- Confirm the selected cover in the platform editor, not only in 齐放Post.
- Topics must be accepted platform suggestions when native topic selection is available.
- Read back requested collection, originality, visibility, save/download permission, scheduling, and any cross-product distribution choices.
- Do not inherit values from a previous draft without checking them.

## WeChat Channels

- Wait for platform processing after transport completes; the percentage alone is insufficient.
- The cover editor may expose a 3:4 main cover plus an additional sharing/card slot. Bind the intended 3:4 cover and, when present, the 4:3 share cover.
- After selecting or uploading the cover, activate the platform modal's Confirm control and verify the modal closes.
- If multiple management-list candidates share a title, use account, time, task fingerprint, and platform status. Do not duplicate-submit to disambiguate.

## Xiaohongshu

- Confirm cover, title/body, and native topic chips.
- Originality is optional and must follow the task's explicit setting; lack of originality permission is not automatically a publishing failure.
- Treat verification, captcha, or risk prompts as user gates.

## Kuaishou

- The cover editor can open on a frame-crop tab even when an uploaded cover exists. Switch to the correct cover source or upload tab when needed.
- Activate the platform modal's Confirm control and verify closure plus the resulting preview.
- Read back topics, collection/originality, visibility, timing, and download permission when those controls are present and requested.

## Shared final readback

For every selected platform, verify:

1. Correct account and video.
2. Upload and processing complete.
3. Intended copy and accepted topics.
4. Intended cover actually bound.
5. Requested platform options.
6. Final publish control present and not already activated.
